History of Poslisshenfd

poslisshenfd is a project I've been working on about a new language that goes across humor and secret conversations. I've began making this 2026-07-21. The language is quite easy to learn. It usually would take 30 to 40 minutes to learn it, since it is using Gaelic like pronunciation and Germanic lettering.
